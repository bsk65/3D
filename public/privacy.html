As the app is operated by me as a private individual with no associated company, I am personally the data controller for the personal data processed in connection with your use of the app.

2. What information we collect

When you create an account and use the app, we collect and process the following personal data:

  • Name
  • Email address
  • Bow type (the type of bow you indicate you shoot with, in your user profile)
  • Gender
  • GPS location data, if you actively choose to enable location tracking (see section 3a)

This information is collected directly from you when you create a user account.

3. Purpose of processing

We process your information for the following purposes:

  • To create and administer your user account
  • To give you access to log in to the app (authentication)
  • To be able to contact you about significant matters concerning your account or the app, if necessary
  • To send you newsletters, if you have given your consent to this (see section 4a)

We do not sell or disclose your information to third parties for their own marketing purposes.

3a. About GPS tracking

The app includes an optional feature that lets you enable GPS tracking. This feature is switched off by default — we only collect your location if you actively turn the feature on.

We use your location to track your route on the course and how long it took. You can turn the feature off again at any time in the app's settings, after which we stop collecting new location data.

We store your location data as a history/track so you can view your previous rounds/shots. You can delete this history in the app, or by contacting us.

4. Legal basis for processing

The processing of data is based on:

  • Article 6(1)(b) of the General Data Protection Regulation (performance of a contract): We need your email and name in order to provide the service (the login function) you have requested by creating an account. The same applies to bow type and gender, to the extent the app's core features (e.g. statistics, comparison, or recommendations tailored to your bow or gender) require this information.
  • Article 6(1)(a) of the General Data Protection Regulation (consent): We process GPS location data solely if you have actively enabled location tracking in the app. You can withdraw this consent at any time by turning the feature off again (see section 3a).
  • Article 6(1)(a) of the General Data Protection Regulation (consent): If you have actively signed up for our newsletter, we process your email for this purpose based on your consent. You can withdraw this consent at any time, without affecting your access to the app otherwise (see section 4a).

4a. About the newsletter

Signing up for the newsletter is a separate and active opt-in — it is not a requirement for using the app or creating an account. You can always unsubscribe from the newsletter again, either via an unsubscribe link in the individual email or by contacting us at bsklausen@proton.me. When you unsubscribe, we stop sending you newsletters, but we may retain the fact that you have unsubscribed, to avoid inadvertently contacting you again.

5. Who has access to your information (data processors)

We use Firebase, a service provided by Google, to operate the app's backend, including login/authentication and data storage. Google acts as a data processor on our behalf in this respect and only processes data according to our instructions.

We have entered into Google's standard "Data Processing and Security Terms" (Firebase/Google Cloud DPA), which satisfies the General Data Protection Regulation's requirements for data processing agreements.

There are no other recipients of your information beyond this, unless required by law (see section 8).

6. Transfer to third countries

Google/Firebase may process data on servers located outside the EU/EEA, including in the USA. Such transfers are covered by the European Commission's standard contractual clauses (SCCs), which Google has adopted, cf. Article 46 of the General Data Protection Regulation.

You can read more about Google's/Firebase's data protection terms at: firebase.google.com/support/privacy

7. Retention and deletion

We retain your information for as long as you have an active account in the app.

If you delete your account, or request that your information be deleted, we will delete your personal data without undue delay and no later than within 30 days, unless we are legally required to retain certain information for longer.

8. Your rights

Under the General Data Protection Regulation, you have the right to:

  • Obtain access to the information we process about you
  • Have inaccurate information corrected
  • Have your information deleted ("the right to be forgotten")
  • Have the processing restricted in certain cases
  • Object to the processing
  • Data portability – receive your information in a structured, commonly used format
  • Withdraw your consent to the newsletter at any time, without affecting the lawfulness of processing based on consent before its withdrawal

If you wish to exercise one or more of these rights, you can contact us at bsklausen@proton.me.

You also have the right to lodge a complaint with the Danish Data Protection Agency (Datatilsynet) if you are dissatisfied with how we process your information:

9. Security

We have taken appropriate technical and organisational measures to protect your information against unauthorised access, alteration, or deletion, including:

  • Encryption of data in transit (HTTPS)
  • Access restrictions to our database
  • Use of Firebase Authentication for secure login handling

10. Changes to this policy

We may update this privacy policy from time to time, for example if the app evolves or the law changes. In the event of significant changes, we will inform you, for example via a notice in the app.
